About Us

Welcome to KIRA Clinic

KIRA Clinic is a trusted private health center located in the heart of Nyagatare District, committed to providing high-quality, patient-centered healthcare services to the community.

Our Mission

To deliver high-quality, affordable, and patient-focused healthcare that promotes the well-being of individuals and families in Nyagatare and beyond.

Our Vision

To be a leading private health center in the Eastern Province, known for excellence in clinical care and community health services.

Core Values

  • Compassion
  • Professionalism
  • Integrity
  • Respect
  • Continuous Improvement
KIRA Clinic — Your Health, Our Priority.

Ibyerekeye KIRA Clinic

Murakaza neza kuri KIRA Clinic

KIRA Clinic ni ivuriro ryigenga rikorera mu Karere ka Nyagatare, ryiyemeje gutanga serivisi z’ubuvuzi bufite ireme kandi zishingiye ku murwayi.

Intego yacu

Gutanga ubuvuzi bufite ireme, buhendutse kandi bwita ku murwayi mu rwego rwo guteza imbere ubuzima bwiza bw’abaturage ba Nyagatare n’ahandi.

Inzozi zacu

Kuba ivuriro ry’icyitegererezo mu Ntara y’Iburasirazuba, rizwiho gutanga serivisi nziza z’ubuvuzi no gufasha abaturage mu bijyanye n’ubuzima.

Indangagaciro zacu

  • Impuhwe
  • Ubunyamwuga
  • Ubumwe n’ubunyangamugayo
  • Kubaha
  • Kwigira hamwe no guhora twiteza imbere
KIRA Clinic — Ubuzima bwawe, ni intego yacu.
